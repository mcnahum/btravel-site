Free version and Btravel Plus

The free version allows up to three non-archived trips. Past trips count until archived. Archiving a trip frees a place once the change is saved. Archived trips remain accessible.

Btravel Plus removes this limit. Three options provide the same unlimited trip access: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription paid in full upfront, or a lifetime one-time purchase. No free trial is offered at launch.

Reference prices for France are EUR 1.99 per month, EUR 14.99 per year, or EUR 49.99 for lifetime access. The price, applicable taxes, currency and terms displayed by the App Store before confirmation apply to your purchase and may vary by country or region.

Payment and renewal

Payments are processed by Apple and charged to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew for the same period unless cancelled before renewal. Apple presents the applicable price; price changes are handled under Apple's rules and applicable law.

You can check your next renewal date and cancel in your Apple subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; access normally continues until the end of the paid period. Deleting Btravel does not cancel a subscription.

Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no renewal or subscription expiry date. It remains subject to the validity of the purchase and the applicable licence; it does not guarantee perpetual availability of the application or third-party services. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel or refund an existing subscription.

Restoration, Family Sharing and end of access

Use Restore Purchases in Btravel with the relevant Apple Account to restore your entitlements. Plus offers support Family Sharing under Apple's terms and service availability. Family Sharing shares Plus access, not your iCloud trips. Shared access may end when sharing is withdrawn.

Expiration, refund or revocation of a Plus entitlement does not delete your trips. You can continue viewing, editing and exporting them. Additions that increase the number of non-archived trips beyond three then require another valid unlimited entitlement. Acquisitions made before the transition to free access receive unlimited trips when their eligibility is verified through the App Store.

Refund requests are handled under Apple's rules and applicable statutory rights: request a refund. Nothing in these terms excludes mandatory refund, warranty or withdrawal rights.
