Support

How can we help?

For help with Btravel, questions, feedback, or a data-related request, contact the developer directly.

Contact

Email: btravel@e-nahum.net
Developer: Marc Nahum
Location: Switzerland

Before contacting support

  • Check that your Apple device and Btravel are up to date.
  • For synchronization issues, confirm that iCloud is enabled for Btravel on your devices.
  • Include the affected device type and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive trip information unless necessary.

Data deletion

You can delete trips and their associated information directly within Btravel. To ask a privacy question or request additional assistance, email us at the address above.

Response time

Support requests are generally reviewed within five business days.
