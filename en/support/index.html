Support

How can we help?

For help with Btravel, questions, feedback, or a data-related request, contact the developer directly.

Contact

Email: btravel@e-nahum.net
Developer: Marc Nahum
Location: Switzerland

Before contacting support

  • Check that your Apple device and Btravel are up to date.
  • For synchronization issues, confirm that iCloud is enabled for Btravel on your devices.
  • Include the affected device type and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive trip information unless necessary.

Data deletion

You can delete trips and their associated information directly within Btravel. To ask a privacy question or request additional assistance, email us at the address above.

Response time

Support requests are generally reviewed within five business days.

Btravel Plus and purchases

The Plus features described here concern the upcoming version 3.0; purchases are not yet available to the public.

Btravel is free for up to three non-archived trips. To create more trips, open Settings → Btravel Plus or try saving a fourth trip. Past trips count until archived; archiving frees a place once saved.

Plus offers a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription paid in full upfront, and a lifetime purchase. All three provide the same unlimited trip access. Subscriptions renew automatically; lifetime access does not renew and does not cancel an existing subscription.

To restore a purchase, open Btravel Plus and choose Restore Purchases with the relevant Apple Account. Cancel subscriptions through Apple settings. Request refunds through Apple. Expiration does not delete your trips or prevent editing or export. Family Sharing shares Plus access, not your private iCloud trips.

If a purchase or historical acquisition is not recognised, contact btravel@e-nahum.net. Never send passwords, payment card details or your complete trip list. See our Terms of Use.
