Privacy Policy

SerieTraktFix helps you review and manage your Trakt TV series without advertising, selling personal data, or tracking you across apps and websites.

Controller

SerieTraktFix is developed and provided by Marc Nahum, Switzerland. For privacy questions, email btravel@e-nahum.net.

Trakt account data

When you authorize your account, the app communicates directly with the Trakt API to read and, only when you request it, modify your shows, seasons, episodes, history, and ratings. Trakt receives the requests needed to provide these features under its own privacy policy.

Authentication and storage

Trakt access tokens are stored in Apple Keychain. The synchronized library, preferences, backups, statistics, and diagnostic log are stored on your devices. Preferences may sync across your devices using iCloud and CloudKit. The developer does not operate a server that receives a copy of your library or credentials.

Images and visual search

SerieTraktFix may download posters and portraits from addresses supplied by Trakt and its metadata providers. Recognition of a selected, pasted, or photographed poster is performed on device using available Apple technologies. The app does not send that image to a server operated by the developer.

Links and third-party services

The app may open Trakt, TMDB, JustWatch, or YouTube to show information, offers, or trailers. Their own privacy policies apply when you use those services. They may receive ordinary technical information such as your IP address and the requested resource.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

SerieTraktFix includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app and cross-website tracking. The developer does not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your devices and in your iCloud space until you or the system removes it. You can revoke SerieTraktFix access from your Trakt account and delete the app to remove its local data. Data retained by Trakt is managed through your Trakt account.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised after a material update.
